# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
GitHub profile README (Markdown + inline HTML subset). Visuals ship as self-made SVG files in `assets/`, generated by `assets/gen.py`, with dark/light variants swapped via `<picture>` + `prefers-color-scheme`. No external scripts, no external fonts inside SVGs (GitHub serves them as `<img>`), CSS/SMIL animation only.

## Users
1. Players and curious developers who land here from fisch.ing, fishfetch or a repo link; their job is to find out who made this and what else there is, then try it or star it.
2. Friends and community who already know Waysel; they visit for fun and to see what's new.

## Product Purpose
The GitHub profile page of Waysel (Waysel07): a front door to their projects. Success = visitors click through to play Fischen! on fisch.ing, and try or star the other repos.

## Positioning
A German hobby developer who makes small, odd, playful things: a pixel-art fishing game that grew out of a joke fish website, a fastfetch that shows a swimming ASCII fish, a Cyberpunk 2077-style Android TV launcher, a glass/glow Windows Terminal theme.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Lead project: **Fischen!** at https://www.fisch.ing (repo is private; link the site, not the repo). Facts from its README: pixel-art canvas fishing game in the browser, no accounts, no install; cast, wait for a bite, keep line tension in the safe range, sell catches for Taler; areas include Steg, Sonneninsel, Frostfjord, Hohe See; Fischdex, shinies, live lobbies, idea board with public votes, blackjack with a card shark (Kartenhai).
- Other public projects: fishfetch (Python), cyberhome (Java, Android TV), terminal-glass-glow (HLSL, Windows Terminal).
- GitHub README rendering limits: no JS, no custom CSS in Markdown, images only; links wrap whole images.

## Brand Commitments
- The pixel goldfish stays as a recognizable mark (user-confirmed).
- Copy in English, with a note that German is fine too (user is German).

## Evidence on Hand
- Live site https://www.fisch.ing. Repo READMEs. No stars or follower numbers worth showing; do not invent stats, player counts or testimonials.

## Product Principles
- Fischen! leads; everything else supports.
- Playful and specific over generic "developer portfolio".
- Every link goes somewhere real.
